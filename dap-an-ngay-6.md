**66.** It was such a funny film that I burst out laughing.  
   (So / such) so + adj ⇔ such + a + adj + N + that.

**68.** Peter suggested that I should sell the shares immediately. / Peter suggested that I sell the shares immediately. / Peter suggested I should sell the shares immediately. / Peter suggested selling the shares immediately. / Peter suggested I sell the shares immediately.

**69.** I asked my mother if she had written a note for Anna. / I asked my mother whether she had written a note for Anna.
