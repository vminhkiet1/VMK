## Viết lại câu

**11.** Sue suggested that Ann should apply for the job. / Sue suggested that Ann apply for the job. / Sue suggested Ann should apply for the job. / Sue suggested Ann apply for the job. / Sue suggested that Ann applied for the job.  
   (Suggest) Why don't you + V? → suggested that + S + (should) + V. Không viết suggested Ann to apply.

**12.** She asked Tom if he was hungry. / She asked Tom whether he was hungry.  
   (Câu gián tiếp) Hỏi Yes/No → asked + người + if/whether + S + V lùi thì (are → was, you → he).

**13.** Gail asked Sally where she had bought her shoes. / Gail asked Sally where she bought her shoes.  
   (Câu gián tiếp) Hỏi Wh-: where + S + V, bỏ did, lùi thì bought → had bought, your → her.

**14.** The teacher told his student not to be late the next day. / The teacher told his student not to be late the following day.  
   (Câu gián tiếp) Mệnh lệnh phủ định → told sb not to V; tomorrow → the next day.

**15.** You'd better go now or you'll be late. / You'd better go now or you will be late. / You'd better go now, or you'll be late.  
   (Had better) advise sb to V ⇔ You'd better + V nguyên mẫu.

#### Viết lại câu

**26.** Despite the good weather, we stayed indoors. / Despite the weather being good, we stayed indoors. / Despite the fact that the weather was good, we stayed indoors.  
   (Liên từ) Although + mệnh đề → Despite + cụm danh từ (the good weather).

**27.** Although he has an English name, he is in fact German. / Although he has an English name, he is German.  
   (Liên từ) In spite of + V-ing → Although + S + V (thêm chủ ngữ he, chia has).

**28.** He is better at football than I am. / He is better at football than me. / He is better than me at football. / He is better than I am at football.  
   (So sánh) not as good as → đổi chủ ngữ, dùng so sánh hơn: better than.

**29.** He is too young to vote.  
   (Too / enough) not old enough ↔ too young (tính từ trái nghĩa).

**30.** Learning a foreign language in a week is impossible.  
   (It + be + adj + to V) It is + adj + to V ⇔ V-ing + … + is + adj.
