## Bài học: Câu gián tiếp + suggest / had better

### Câu gián tiếp: 4 loại câu

| Loại câu | Trực tiếp | Gián tiếp | Mẹo |
|---|---|---|---|
| Câu kể | She said, "I am tired." | She said (that) she was tired. | said (that) + lùi thì. said to sb → told sb. |
| Hỏi Yes/No | "Are you hungry, Tom?" | She asked Tom if / whether he was hungry. | asked + if/whether + S + V. KHÔNG đảo. |
| Hỏi Wh- | "Where do you live?" | He asked me where I lived. | asked + wh- + S + V. Bỏ do/does/did, KHÔNG đảo. |
| Mệnh lệnh | "Don't be late." | He told me not to be late. | told / asked / warned + sb + (not) to V. |

### Lùi thì và đổi từ chỉ thời gian

| Trực tiếp | Gián tiếp |
|---|---|
| am / is / are · V(s) · will · can | was / were · V2 · would · could |
| V2 · have/has + V3 | had + V3 |
| now · today · tonight | then · that day · that night |
| tomorrow · yesterday · next week | the next day (the following day) · the day before · the following week |
| here · this · these | there · that · those |

### Gợi ý và khuyên

| Câu gốc | Viết lại | Mẹo |
|---|---|---|
| "Why don't you apply for the job, Ann?" said Sue. | Sue suggested that Ann (should) apply for the job. | suggest + that + S + (should) + V nguyên mẫu, hoặc suggest + V-ing. KHÔNG có suggest sb to V. |
| Let's stay in this evening. | Why don't we stay in this evening? | Let's + V = Why don't we + V? |
| You should leave now. | You'd better leave now. | had better + V nguyên mẫu; phủ định: had better not + V. |
| If I were you, I would rest. | I advise you to rest. | advise sb (not) to V. |

### Cấu tạo từ: nhìn vị trí chỗ trống

| Vị trí | Cần loại từ | Ví dụ |
|---|---|---|
| Sau a / the / my / his, hoặc làm chủ ngữ | Danh từ (-tion, -ment, -ness, -ity, -ence, -ance) | make a big difference · his success |
| Trước danh từ; sau be / become / feel / seem | Tính từ (-ful, -less, -ous, -ive, -able, -al) | an energetic person · It becomes flexible. |
| Bổ nghĩa cho động từ hoặc tính từ | Trạng từ (-ly) | work productively · extremely high |
| Nghĩa ngược lại | Tiền tố un-, im-, in-, dis-, ir- | impatient · dishonest · uncomfortable |
| -ed / -ing | -ed: người cảm thấy · -ing: vật gây ra cảm giác | I'm bored. · The film is boring. |

### Tìm lỗi sai: soát 6 chỗ theo thứ tự

| Soát | Lỗi hay gặp trong đề trường | Sửa |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Thì, chia động từ | warned that a storm approached (đang tới) | was approaching |
| 2. Loại từ | becomes extremely flexibly · as frequent as possible | flexible · frequently |
| 3. Liên từ | despite she wasn't · Although …, but … | although · bỏ but |
| 4. Số ít / nhiều, đếm được | every fruits · the number of homework | every fruit · the amount of |
| 5. Mạo từ | a MP · only part of information | an MP · the information |
| 6. Đại từ quan hệ | Ginger capsules, that are sold… | which (có dấu phẩy → không dùng that) |

### Ghi nhớ

- Câu hỏi gián tiếp là câu kể: không có dấu hỏi, không đảo trợ động từ.
- Động từ tường thuật ở hiện tại (says, asks) thì KHÔNG lùi thì.
- said + (to sb) + that…; told + sb + that… Không nói said me, không nói told that.
- Phần Tìm lỗi sai: mỗi câu chỉ có 1 lỗi. Đọc cả câu, rồi soát 4 chỗ gạch chân theo 6 bước trên. Không chắc thì chọn chỗ có vấn đề về loại từ hoặc liên từ trước.

### Bẫy hay gặp

**1. Đảo trợ động từ trong câu hỏi gián tiếp**
- ❌ He asked me where did I live.
- ✅ He asked me where I lived.
- Gián tiếp là câu kể: S + V, bỏ did, lùi thì live → lived.

**2. suggest sb to V**
- ❌ He suggested me to go home.
- ✅ He suggested that I (should) go home. / He suggested going home.
- suggest không đi với sb + to V. (advise / tell / ask thì đi được: advised me to go.)

**3. said me**
- ❌ She said me that she was busy.
- ✅ She told me that she was busy. / She said to me that…
- tell + người; say + (to người).

### The Origin of Jeans (Test 1 · 11–17)

(1) In 1853, the California gold rush was in full swing and everyday items were in short supply. Levi Strauss, a 24-year-old German immigrant, left New York for San Francisco with a small supply of dry goods with the intention of opening a branch of his brother's New York dry goods business. Shortly after his arrival, a prospector wanted to know what Mr. Strauss was selling. When Strauss told him he had rough canvas cloth used for tents and wagon covers, the prospector said, 'You should have brought trousers!', because he couldn't find a pair of trousers strong enough to last.

(2) Levi Strauss had the canvas made into work trousers. Miners liked the trousers, but complained that the fabric was not very comfortable and tended to chafe. Levi Strauss substituted the canvas with a twilled cotton cloth from France called 'serge de Nimes.' The fabric later became known as denim and the trousers were nicknamed blue jeans. Then Levi Strauss and Nevada tailor David Jacobs co-patented the process of putting small metal rivets in places such as the sides of the pockets that needed extra strength. On May 20, 1873, they received U.S. Patent No. 139,121. This date is now considered the official birthday of 'blue jeans.'

(3) But why were they called blue jeans? Denim is unique in its singular connection with one colour. It is made from thread that is traditionally dyed with blue pigment obtained from indigo dye. Indigo was linked with practical fabrics and work clothing. The durability of indigo as a colour and its darkness of tone made it a good choice when frequent washing was not possible.
