## Bài học: Câu điều kiện + unless + câu ước

### Câu điều kiện: nhìn mệnh đề chính để chọn loại

| Loại | Dùng khi | Mệnh đề If | Mệnh đề chính | Ví dụ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | sự thật, quy luật | If + hiện tại đơn | hiện tại đơn | If you heat ice, it melts. |
| 1 | có thể xảy ra (hiện tại / tương lai) | If + hiện tại đơn | will / can + V | If it rains, I will stay at home. |
| 2 | không có thật ở HIỆN TẠI | If + V2 / were | would / could + V | If I were you, I would study harder. |
| 3 | không có thật trong QUÁ KHỨ | If + had + V3 | would / could have + V3 | If I had studied, I would have passed. |

### Câu ước

| Ước về | Công thức | Ví dụ (thực tế) |
|---|---|---|
| Hiện tại | S + wish + S + V2 / were | I wish I had a car. (thực tế: không có xe) |
| Quá khứ | S + wish + S + had + V3 | I wish I had gone to the party. (thực tế: đã không đi) |
| Muốn ai / cái gì thay đổi | S + wish + S + would + V | I wish it would stop raining. |

### 4 mẫu viết lại câu hay ra

| Câu gốc | Viết lại | Mẹo |
|---|---|---|
| If you don't hurry, you will be late. | Unless you hurry, you will be late. | unless = if … not → BỎ not. |
| I don't have time, so I can't help you. | If I had time, I could help you. | Thật ở hiện tại → loại 2: lùi 1 thì và đổi phủ định ↔ khẳng định. |
| She didn't study, so she failed. | If she had studied, she wouldn't have failed. | Thật ở quá khứ → loại 3: had V3 / would have V3, đổi phủ định ↔ khẳng định. |
| I'm sorry I didn't call her. / It's a pity I can't swim. | I wish I had called her. / I wish I could swim. | Tiếc về quá khứ → wish + had V3. Tiếc ở hiện tại → wish + V2 / could. |

### Ghi nhớ

- Mẹo "lùi 1 thì": thực tế ở hiện tại → dùng V2; thực tế ở quá khứ → dùng had + V3. Luôn đổi khẳng định ↔ phủ định.
- Loại 2 và câu ước dùng were cho mọi ngôi: If I were…, I wish she were…
- Nhìn mệnh đề chính để biết loại: will → loại 1; would + V → loại 2; would have + V3 → loại 3.
- It takes + sb + thời gian + to V ⇔ S + spend(s) + thời gian + V-ing. Giữ đúng thì: It took → spent.
- as long as / provided that = miễn là (nghĩa giống if).

### Bẫy hay gặp

**1. Dùng will trong mệnh đề if**
- ❌ If it will rain tomorrow, we will stay at home.
- ✅ If it rains tomorrow, we will stay at home.
- Mệnh đề if (cũng như when, before…) không dùng will; dùng hiện tại đơn.

**2. Unless + not**
- ❌ Unless you don't study, you will fail.
- ✅ Unless you study, you will fail.
- Unless đã có nghĩa phủ định (= if not). Thêm not sẽ thành phủ định 2 lần.

**3. Đặt would have ở cả hai vế**
- ❌ If I would have known, I would have helped.
- ✅ If I had known, I would have helped.
- Vế if của loại 3 luôn là had + V3. would chỉ ở vế còn lại.

## Luyện thêm: thì + where / which

Ôn tiếp 2 lỗi lặp lại (mốc quá khứ → không dùng have; câu chủ động → không có been) và lỗi where/which của ngày 2. Mẹo where/which: sau chỗ trống có S + V ĐỦ nghĩa → where; S + V còn THIẾU tân ngữ, hoặc ngay sau là động từ → which.

#### Thì + mệnh đề quan hệ

**1.** We ___ three units of the book so far.

A. finished   B. had finished   C. have finished   D. have been finished

**2.** They ___ married in 2015.

A. got   B. have got   C. had got   D. have been got

**3.** The new library ___ by the mayor last month.

A. has opened   B. has been opened   C. opened   D. was opened

**4.** I ___ English for six years, but I still find speaking difficult.

A. learned   B. have learned   C. had learned   D. have been learned

**5.** When I got home, I realised I ___ my phone at the office.

A. have left   B. had left   C. left   D. was leaving

**6.** She ___ in Da Nang when she was a student.

A. lived   B. has lived   C. has been lived   D. had been living

**7.** Many trees ___ in our town since last year.

A. have planted   B. planted   C. have been planted   D. were planting

**8.** He ___ to work by motorbike every day last year.

A. has gone   B. had gone   C. has been going   D. went

**9.** Da Lat, ___ is famous for its flowers, attracts many tourists.

A. which   B. where   C. that   D. who

**10.** This is the café ___ I often meet my friends.

A. which   B. that   C. where   D. whom

### Volunteering: Good for Others, Good for You

(1) Every year, millions of people around the world give their time to help others without being paid. They clean beaches, teach children, cook meals for the homeless or visit elderly people who live alone. This kind of unpaid work is called volunteering.

(2) Most volunteers say they started because they wanted to help their community. However, research shows that volunteering also brings many benefits to the volunteers themselves. People who volunteer regularly often feel happier and less stressed. Some studies have even found that older volunteers tend to live longer than people of the same age who do not volunteer.

(3) Volunteering can also be useful for young people who are looking for a job. It gives them a chance to develop skills such as teamwork, communication and problem-solving. Many employers say that they are more interested in candidates who have done voluntary work, because it shows that they are responsible and willing to learn.

(4) Of course, volunteering is not always easy. Some volunteers find it hard to balance their volunteer work with their studies or their job. Others feel disappointed when they cannot see the results of their work immediately. Experts suggest that new volunteers should start with just a few hours a month and choose an activity they really enjoy.

(5) If more people gave just a little of their free time, many communities would become stronger and kinder places to live.

## Luyện nói: Gia đình + Sở thích

### Bài mẫu: Your family (khoảng 1 phút, điền chỗ trống)

> There are ___ people in my family: my ___, my ___ and me.
> My father is a ___ and my mother works as a ___.
> I'm closest to my ___ because ___.
> We usually ___ together at the weekend.
> If I had more free time, I would ___ with my family more often.
> I wish I could ___.

**Khung trả lời:** Ý chính → Lý do (because…) → Ví dụ (For example…)

**Câu cứu nguy:**

- *That's an interesting question. Let me think…*
- *Could you repeat the question, please?*
- *I'm not sure, but I think…*
- *What I mean is…*

### Câu hỏi của giám khảo

1. **Can you tell me about your family?**  
   Gợi ý: Số người, mỗi người làm gì. There are four people in my family…
2. **Who are you closest to in your family? Why?**  
   Gợi ý: Ý chính → because… → For example, when I was…, she…
3. **If you could change one thing about your family life, what would it be?**  
   Gợi ý: Điều kiện loại 2: If I could…, I would… Ví dụ: spend more time with my parents.
4. **What do you usually do in your free time?**  
   Gợi ý: Hiện tại đơn + usually / often. 1 sở thích → vì sao thích → ví dụ.
5. **What hobby did you have as a child? Do you still do it?**  
   Gợi ý: Quá khứ đơn cho hồi nhỏ (I played…). Tới bây giờ: I have played it for… / I gave it up when… Câu chủ động: không có been.
6. **If you had more free time, what would you do?**  
   Gợi ý: If I had more free time, I would… because… Có thể dùng I wish I had more time to…

**Tiêu chí chấm:** Trôi chảy (ít ngập ngừng, nói đủ ý); Ngữ pháp (thì đúng, câu điều kiện, câu ước); Từ vựng (đúng chủ đề, dùng đúng các cặp từ dễ nhầm); Phát âm (âm cuối -s, -ed, trọng âm); Nội dung (trả lời đúng câu hỏi, có lý do và ví dụ).
