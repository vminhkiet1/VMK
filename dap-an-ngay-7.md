#### Viết lại câu

**33.** If she had taken the medicine, she would have got better. / If she had taken the medicine, she would have gotten better.  
   (Câu điều kiện) Thực tế quá khứ → loại 3: had + V3, would have + V3, đổi cả 2 vế sang khẳng định. (Lần trước bạn viết: If she taken the medicine, she had getten better.)

**34.** I spend 30 minutes getting to work. / I spend thirty minutes getting to work.  
   (It takes / spend) It takes sb + time + to V ⇔ S + spend + time + V-ing. Hiện tại → spend (không phải spent). (Lần trước bạn viết: I spend 30 minutes to get to work.)

**35.** I have worked here for two years. / I have been working here for two years.  
   (Hiện tại hoàn thành) started … ago → have + V3 … for + khoảng thời gian. (Lần trước bạn viết: I have been started working here two years ago..)

**36.** A new school will be built next year.  
   (Bị động) Tương lai đơn bị động: will be + V3. Bỏ 'by them'. (Lần trước bạn viết: A new school will be built a new school next year..)

**37.** It is said that he is very rich.  
   (Bị động) People say that … → It is said that … (bị động với động từ tường thuật). (Lần trước bạn viết: It is said he was very rich.)

**38.** I haven't seen her since 2020.  
   (Hiện tại hoàn thành) The last time + S + V2 + was + mốc → S + haven't + V3 + since + mốc. (Lần trước bạn viết: I haven't been seen her was since 2020.)

**39.** He hasn't called me for two months. / He hasn't called me for two months now.  
   (Hiện tại hoàn thành) It's + khoảng thời gian + since + S + last + V2 → S + hasn't + V3 + for + khoảng thời gian. (Lần trước bạn viết: He hasn't been last called me for two months.)

**40.** I have learned English for five years. / I have learnt English for five years. / I have been learning English for five years.  
   (Hiện tại hoàn thành) started … ago → have + V3 (hoặc have been + V-ing) + for + khoảng thời gian. Bỏ "ago". (Lần trước bạn viết: I have been learned English for five years.)

**41.** My brother has worked in Hanoi for two years. / My brother has been working in Hanoi for two years.  
   (Hiện tại hoàn thành) started … ago → has + V3 (hoặc has been + V-ing) + for. Không viết has been worked. (Lần trước bạn viết: My brother has started working for two years in Hanoi.)

**42.** If I had a car, I could drive to work. / If I had a car, I would be able to drive to work. / If I had a car, I would drive to work.  
   (Câu điều kiện) Thực tế hiện tại → loại 2: don't have → had; can't → could (đổi phủ định thành khẳng định). (Lần trước bạn viết: If I had a car, I drove to work.)

**43.** She hasn't seen him since she was ten.  
   (Ago / since / for) last + V2 → hasn't + V3 + since. Không có been.

**44.** They last wrote emails two months ago. / They last wrote an email two months ago. / They last wrote email two months ago.  
   (Ago / since / for) haven't V3 for … → last + V2 + … ago.

**45.** If my brother had had enough money, he would have bought that car.  
   (Câu điều kiện) Quá khứ → loại 3: had had (đúng, 2 chữ had) … would have bought.

**46.** If he spoke clearly, people would understand him.  
   (Câu điều kiện) Hiện tại → loại 2: spoke … would understand.

**47.** I wish I had known that she was ill. / I wish I had known she was ill.  
   (Câu ước) Tiếc về quá khứ → wish + had V3.

**48.** The meeting has been cancelled. / The meeting has been cancelled by them.  
   (Bị động) Hiện tại hoàn thành bị động: has been + V3 (vật → có been).

**49.** I got an engineer to repair my computer a week ago.  
   (Have sth done) get + người + to V (nhờ ai làm). have + người + V; have + vật + V3.

**50.** They spent 5 months building the house. / They spent five months building the house.  
   (It takes / spend) It took + sb + time + to V → sb + spent + time + V-ing.

**51.** It is 2 years since Nam last went to Ha Noi. / It is two years since Nam last went to Ha Noi. / It is 2 years since Nam was last in Ha Noi. / It is 2 years since Nam went to Ha Noi. / It is two years since Nam went to Ha Noi.  
   (It is … since) haven't V3 for + time → It is + time + since + S + (last) + V2.

**52.** The older he grows, the more cheerful he becomes. / The older he gets, the more cheerful he becomes.  
   (So sánh kép) The + so sánh hơn + S + V, the + so sánh hơn + S + V.

**53.** It was such a difficult question that no one could answer it.  
   (So / such) so + adj ⇔ such + a + adj + N.

**54.** Because of the driver's carelessness, the car crashed. / Because of the careless driver, the car crashed.  
   (Liên từ) because + mệnh đề → because of + cụm danh từ (carelessness / the careless driver).
